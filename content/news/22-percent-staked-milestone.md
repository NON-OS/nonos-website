## Why This Model Works

### 1. Sustainable Emissions
Total emissions are capped at 40M NOX (~5% of supply). Unlike infinite inflation models, NOX staking has a defined endpoint. After Year 2, emissions stop entirely.

### 2. Front-Loaded Incentives
Higher Year 1 rewards attract early adopters. As the ecosystem matures, declining emissions create scarcity while existing stakers compound their positions.

### 3. Time-Weighted Rewards
The lock boost system rewards commitment. A staker willing to lock for 365 days earns 2.5x more than a flexible staker—fair compensation for reduced liquidity.

### 4. NFT Utility Integration
ZeroState Pass NFTs provide permanent, transferable boost utility. This creates intrinsic value beyond speculation and establishes a price floor tied to staking economics.

### 5. Deflationary Pressure
Early unlock penalties are burned (sent to 0xdead), permanently reducing supply. Every early exit benefits remaining holders.

### 6. Aligned Incentives
Stakers benefit when the ecosystem grows. Higher NOX value + staking rewards creates a positive feedback loop between community and protocol development.
