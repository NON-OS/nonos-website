## Staking V3 - Multi-Position & Early Unlock

We're excited to announce **NOX Staking V3** is now live on Ethereum mainnet. This upgrade introduces powerful new features while preserving all existing stakes and rewards.

---

### What's New in V3

**Multi-Position Staking**
- Create up to **10 separate stake positions**
- Each position can have its own lock period
- Manage your staking strategy with precision
- Perfect for DCA staking or testing different lock periods

**Early Unlock**
- Exit locked positions **before** the lock expires
- Pay a **5% penalty fee** (burned to reduce supply)
- Penalty tokens are permanently removed from circulation

**Automatic Migration**
- Your existing stake is **automatically migrated** when you interact
- All rewards are preserved - nothing is lost
- No action required if you're happy with current position

---

### How to Use Early Unlock

1. Go to [nonos.software/staking](https://nonos.software/staking)
2. Connect your wallet
3. Scroll to **"Your Positions"** section
4. Click **"Early Exit"** on any locked position
5. Confirm the transaction

Early unlock incurs a **5% penalty** which is burned. This is helpful for anyone who locked tokens and needs immediate liquidity.

### FAQ

**Q: Are my rewards safe?**
A: Yes. All pending rewards are preserved during migration.

**Q: Do I need to do anything?**
A: No. Your stake migrates automatically when you stake, unstake, or claim.

**Q: What happens to penalties?**
A: Penalty tokens are burned (sent to 0xdead), reducing total supply.

**Q: Can I still stake without a lock?**
A: Yes. No-lock staking still works exactly as before.

**Q: What is the early unlock penalty?**
A: 5% of your staked amount is burned when using early unlock.
