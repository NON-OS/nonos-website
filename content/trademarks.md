title: "Trademarks and brand use"
description: "How the NØNOS and NOX names and logos may be used. The code is AGPL; the marks identify the genuine project."
---

The NØNOS software is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0. That license
governs the **code**. It does not grant rights to the **names and logos**. Those
marks exist so that people can tell the genuine project from a fork, a
repackage, or an imitation, and this page explains how to use them.

The intent is simple and friendly: talk about NØNOS all you want, link to us,
teach it, review it, build on it. Just do not use our name or logo in a way that
implies we published, endorsed, or vouch for something we did not.

## The marks

"NØNOS", "NONOS", "NOX", the NØNOS logomark, and the NØNOS wordmark are marks of
the NØNOS project (NON-OS). Using them means the guidance below applies.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to refer to the project truthfully: "runs on NØNOS", "a NØNOS
  capsule", "compatible with NØNOS", "built with the NOX SDK".
- Link to the project and its documentation.
- Write about, review, teach, and criticise NØNOS, in any medium.
- Use the logo unmodified to link back to the official site or to credit the
  project, at a legible size, with clear space around it.
- Redistribute unmodified official builds and keep the marks on them.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the name or logo as, or as part of, the name of your product, company,
  service, token, or domain, in a way that suggests it is an official NØNOS
  offering.
- Modify the logo, or use a confusingly similar name or mark.
- Put the marks on a modified build, a fork, or a repackage in a way that
  implies it is the official, unmodified NØNOS. Rename your fork and say what it
  is based on instead.
- Imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or affiliation that does not
  exist, including in fundraising, token sales, or marketing.

## Forks and modified builds

Forking is welcome; the license guarantees it. If you ship a modified build,
give it a distinct name and make clear it is derived from NØNOS and not the
official release. This protects your users, who need to know whether the trust
chain they are relying on is the one the project stands behind.

## The NOX token

The NOX name and marks follow the same rules. NOX staking grants ecosystem
rights and does not grant kernel capabilities; do not use NØNOS or NOX branding
to imply that a token, a sale, or a third-party service is endorsed by the
project or that it confers authority on a NØNOS machine.

## Questions

If a use is not covered here, or you want permission for something this page
withholds, ask. Reasonable requests that do not create confusion are usually
fine.

This page is guidance, not a contract, and may be updated. When in doubt, the
test is simple: would a reasonable person think the project made or endorsed
this? If yes, and we did not, do not.
