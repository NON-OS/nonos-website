NØNOS is built in the open, and it is shown in the open. This is where the
project appears: the launch, talks, and sessions where you can watch a verified
operating system boot and prove itself.

## Version 1.0 launch

**July 22, 2026.** Version 1.0 ships: the signature-verified image, the live
in-browser boot, the full documentation, and the reproducible-build and
verification tooling, all public. The launch is online and open; the best way to
mark it is to [boot NØNOS live](/download/) the day it lands and read the
[verification](/docs/architecture/verification/) page to see exactly what is
proven.

## Talks and sessions

As talks and community sessions are scheduled, they are listed here with dates
and links. If you run a conference, a meetup, or a podcast and want a walkthrough
of the capsule model, the attestation gate, or the proof system, get in touch.
