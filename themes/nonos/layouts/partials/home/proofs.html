Not audited. Proven.

Audits sample; proofs cover. Lean 4 states what must be true, the shipping Rust is tied to that spec by machine-checked extraction with Charon and Aeneas, Verus and Kani prove the implementation over every input, and the primitives underneath are pinned to NIST and RFC vectors.

The proofs compile against the kernel source itself, never a copy, so a divergence is a build error. Writing them found and fixed two real bugs in the kernel's ZK verifier.
